# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers to clarify requirements and acceptance criteria
- Works with Project Managers on estimation, planning, and technical risk identification
- Partners with Data Privacy Officer to implement privacy-by-design principles
- Works with Agile Coach on technical practices and process improvements
- Coordinates with Vendor Liaison on technical integration of vendor components
- Collaborates with User Research Specialist during design and prototyping phases

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Works closely with Developers to clarify requirements and validate solutions
- Aligns with Project Managers on roadmap timing and resource allocation
- Partners with User Research Specialist to incorporate research findings into requirements
- Collaborates with Change Management Lead to understand impact on users and business processes
- Works with Vendor Liaison to clarify vendor requirements and acceptance criteria
- Partners with Agile Coach on backlog refinement and prioritization

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Coordinates with Developers on estimation, planning, and managing technical risks
- Aligns with Product Managers on roadmap timing and scope management
- Works with Data Privacy Officer to identify privacy requirements early
- Partners with Change Management Lead to align change activities with project milestones
- Collaborates with Vendor Liaison to schedule and integrate vendor deliverables
- Partners with User Research Specialist to schedule research activities within project timelines
- Works with Agile Coach on agile delivery approaches and process improvements

---

## Data Privacy Officer

### Role Summary
Ensures project compliance with data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA, etc.) and works directly with legal, IT, and security teams to protect sensitive data throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review project requirements and designs for privacy implications
- Conduct Privacy Impact Assessments (PIAs) for projects handling personal data
- Ensure compliance with data protection regulations and company policies
- Advise on data minimization, retention, and deletion practices
- Coordinate with legal and security teams on privacy incidents
- Provide privacy training and guidance to project teams

### Goals
- Maintain regulatory compliance and avoid privacy violations
- Build privacy considerations into project planning from the start
- Minimize organizational risk related to data handling

### Typical Communication
- Privacy impact assessments and compliance reports
- Design review feedback on data flows and storage
- Coordination with Risk Manager and Security teams
- Privacy requirement specifications for development teams

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Works with Project Managers to identify privacy requirements early
- Reviews technical designs with Developers to ensure privacy-by-design
- Collaborates with Risk Manager on privacy-related risks
- Partners with Change Management Lead on privacy training initiatives

---

## Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Facilitates agile adoption, supports scrum teams, and mentors project managers on iterative delivery practices. Helps teams embrace agile values and continuously improve their processes.

### Responsibilities
- Coach teams on agile principles, ceremonies, and best practices
- Facilitate sprint planning, retrospectives, and daily standups
- Help remove impediments and foster self-organization
- Mentor Project Managers and Scrum Masters
- Assess team maturity and guide process improvements
- Promote continuous learning and experimentation

### Goals
- Enable teams to deliver value iteratively and incrementally
- Improve team velocity and predictability over time
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and collaboration

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of agile ceremonies and workshops
- Coaching conversations with team members and leaders
- Retrospective insights and improvement action plans
- Team health assessments and maturity models

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Mentors Project Managers on agile delivery approaches
- Coaches Developers on technical practices like TDD and pair programming
- Partners with Product Managers on backlog refinement and prioritization
- Supports Change Management Lead in organizational agile transformations

---

## Vendor Liaison

### Role Summary
Acts as the primary point of contact for external vendors and third-party service providers. Clarifies requirements, ensures timely deliverables, resolves issues, and manages vendor relationships throughout the project.

### Responsibilities
- Serve as main contact between internal teams and external vendors
- Communicate project requirements and expectations to vendors
- Track vendor deliverables and ensure quality standards are met
- Coordinate vendor onboarding and integration activities
- Escalate and resolve vendor-related issues
- Manage vendor contracts and service level agreements (SLAs)

### Goals
- Ensure vendor deliverables meet quality and timeline expectations
- Maintain positive vendor relationships for long-term success
- Minimize project delays caused by vendor dependencies

### Typical Communication
- Weekly vendor sync meetings and status reports
- Requirements specifications and technical handoffs
- Issue escalation and resolution tracking
- Contract reviews and SLA monitoring reports

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborates with Project Managers to schedule and integrate vendor deliverables
- Works with Developers to coordinate technical integration of vendor components
- Partners with Product Managers to clarify vendor requirements and acceptance criteria
- Coordinates with Risk Manager on vendor-related risks and dependencies

---

## User Research Specialist

### Role Summary
Conducts user research and feedback sessions to ensure end-user needs are represented in project planning and execution. Provides insights that inform product decisions and validate solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Design and conduct user research studies (interviews, surveys, usability tests)
- Analyze research findings and synthesize insights
- Create user personas, journey maps, and experience artifacts
- Validate product assumptions and prototypes with real users
- Advocate for user needs in planning and design discussions
- Share research insights with product and development teams

### Goals
- Ensure products are designed with user needs and behaviors in mind
- Reduce risk of building features that don't meet user expectations
- Validate assumptions early to save time and resources

### Typical Communication
- Research reports and insight presentations
- User personas and journey maps
- Usability test findings and recommendations
- Participation in design reviews and planning sessions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to incorporate findings into requirements and roadmaps
- Collaborates with Developers during design and prototyping phases
- Partners with Project Managers to schedule research activities within project timelines
- Shares insights with Change Management Lead to inform training and adoption strategies

---

## Change Management Lead

### Role Summary
Plans and manages communication, training, and readiness activities for organizational change throughout the project lifecycle. Ensures smooth adoption of new processes, systems, or ways of working.

### Responsibilities
- Develop change management strategies and plans
- Assess change readiness and identify resistance points
- Create communication plans for stakeholders at all levels
- Design and deliver training programs for end users
- Monitor adoption metrics and address concerns
- Coordinate with leadership on organizational alignment

### Goals
- Maximize user adoption and minimize resistance to change
- Ensure stakeholders are informed and prepared for transitions
- Reduce productivity loss during organizational changes

### Typical Communication
- Change management plans and readiness assessments
- Stakeholder communication campaigns and newsletters
- Training materials and user guides
- Adoption metrics and feedback reports

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partners with Project Managers to align change activities with project milestones
- Works with Product Managers to understand impact on users and business processes
- Collaborates with User Research Specialist to understand user concerns and needs
- Coordinates with Data Privacy Officer on privacy-related training and communications
- Supports Agile Coach in organizational agile transformations
